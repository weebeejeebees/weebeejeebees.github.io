Business Overview & Original Project Summary

This digital brochure presents the ERP proposal for Lahvada Laundromat, a thriving laundry service located in Davao City. Our previous system design from the Project Management course primarily focused on automating Lahvada's existing manual transaction recording, sales reporting, and employee attendance tracking to enhance their operational efficiency.

ERP System Chosen

For this proposal, we have chosen Odoo as the Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system. Odoo offers a fully integrated suite of modules perfectly suited to the needs of a growing service business.

Purpose of This Brochure

The primary purpose of this brochure is to clearly demonstrate to business owners and decision-makers how embracing an ERP like Odoo can significantly enhance Lahvada Laundromat's system efficiency, provide crucial data-driven insights, and support sustainable business growth beyond our initial custom solution.

Key Odoo Modules Utilized

  • Odoo Point of Sale (POS): For automated transaction recording and digital receipts.
  • Odoo Inventory: For efficient tracking of supplies and machine status.
  • Odoo Employees: For streamlined employee management and accurate attendance tracking.
  • Odoo Accounting: For comprehensive financial reporting and real-time sales insights.

Alignment with Business Needs

These modules are meticulously designed to replace Lahvada's current manual processes, directly addressing their need for improved data accuracy and accessibility. By providing real-time sales insights, streamlining supply management, simplifying employee records, and delivering comprehensive financial reporting, Odoo perfectly aligns with Lahvada's objectives to enhance operational efficiency and support long-term business growth.
